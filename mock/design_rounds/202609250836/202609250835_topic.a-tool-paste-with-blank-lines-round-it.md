# a-tool-paste-with-blank-lines-round-it

A capture taken on 2026-09-25 quoted the self-compact injector's own
`/compact "..."` line as the person's words. The injector had recorded it in
`<session>.typed-by-tools.ndjson` at 04:34:58Z, and the typed line landed
at 04:35:17Z, well inside the sixty seconds, so the time bound was not what
let it through.

The words were the recorded text inside one `<pasted_content id="6855">`
wrapper, and the wrapper had two newlines in front of it and one after it:

```text
"\n\n<pasted_content id=\"6855\">\n/compact \"Keep: ...\"\n</pasted_content id=\"6855\">\n"
```

`unwrapped` strips the wrapper only when it opens and closes the words
exactly, so this did not match and the line stayed the person's. The harness
puts those line breaks there itself; the person typed nothing around the paste.

The fix keeps the strictness the tests pin: words with anything else around the
wrapper, a space in front of a bare text, or the wrapper nested, still do not
match. Only line breaks on the outside of one whole wrapper are the harness's,
and they are allowed. A bare text with line breaks round it stays the person's,
since nothing says the harness adds them there.
