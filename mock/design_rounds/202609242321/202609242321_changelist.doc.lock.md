## Current state

The capture paragraphs of `mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl` let a typed-by-tools record take
a matching line stamped any time after its `at`, name the project directory's
spelling without saying how characters are counted, and say the context around
a message is summarised once muisti builds where homma runs.

## Changes proposed

- A record takes a line stamped at or after its `at` and no more than sixty
  seconds after it, with the reason: the record is written before the typing
  and nothing confirms the typing landed.
- Characters in the project directory's name are counted in UTF-16 units, so a
  character outside the basic plane is two dashes.
- The sentence about a later summary goes.
