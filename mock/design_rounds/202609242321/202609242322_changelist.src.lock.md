# src changelist

Implements `202609242321_changelist.doc.lock.md`.

## Changes

- CHANGE `capture::typed::without`: a record takes a said event only when the
  event's stamp is at or after `at` and no more than `typed::WINDOW`, sixty
  seconds, after it. A test plants a record whose line never landed and the
  person's same words two minutes later, and asserts the words are kept; a test
  plants two records and two lines of the same text and asserts both are
  dropped, pinning one record to one line.
- CHANGE `capture::store::escaped`: a character outside `[A-Za-z0-9]` becomes as
  many `-` as it has UTF-16 units. A test over an emoji path asserts two.
- `docs/DESIGN.md` re-renders from the template through `cargo mock`.
