# doc changelist

Topic: 202611262324_topic.a-brief-is-said-once-and-bounded.md

## CHANGE: when a brief row is due FROM once per text TO once per ttl whatever the text

`mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, section `## A nudge`, the `brief = true` paragraph and the record paragraph.

- Was: a brief row is due for each new text in a session, and again for the same text after
  `ttl`; the record keeps the digest of the text last briefed.
- Now: a brief row is due as any reminder is, once in a session and again after `ttl`, whatever
  the text. A brief at another time is asked of `homma what --brief -`. An empty text or an
  empty brief is not counted as said. A brief that cannot be made is said once, with the reason
  and the command that makes it by hand. The record keeps no digest.
- Why: every distinct prompt was briefed, so the context grew by a brief with every message.

## CHANGE: what a brief keeps of the answer FROM any substring and any placed line TO whole lines and one reason

`mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, section `### A brief of what bears on a unit of work`, the checking paragraph.

- Was: a quote is kept where it is any substring of its lines, and every placed line with no
  quote is a reason of its thing.
- Now: a quote is kept only where it is a whole line, or the whole of the lines named, with
  whitespace folded and leading list, heading and quote marks and emphasis marks set aside. The
  one reason is the words of the line placed at the thing's heading; any other line without a
  quote is dropped. Three quotes a thing at most.
- Why: a fragment can leave out a "never", and a reason from a body line is unchecked paraphrase.

## CHANGE: how much a brief prints FROM unbounded TO 3000 bytes

`mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, section `### A brief of what bears on a unit of work`, a new paragraph after the checking one.

- Was: `--limit` bounded the things named and nothing bounded their reasons, quotes or the whole.
- Now: the printed brief is at most 3000 bytes; things are kept in order while the whole fits
  and those left out are counted on one line naming `homma what <words>` and a smaller `--limit`.
- Why: a brief said into an agent's context must have a known size.
