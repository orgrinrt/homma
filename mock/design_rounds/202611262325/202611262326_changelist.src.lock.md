## CHANGE: a brief row's due FROM once per text TO once per ttl

`crates/homma-engine/src/cmd/nudge/mod.rs`, `crates/homma-engine/src/cmd/nudge/record.rs`

- The `Inform` arm skips any row said within its `ttl`, a brief row included; the digest of the
  text and the new-work override are gone, and so is `RowState::briefed`.
- A brief row whose brief is empty, or whose text is, records nothing, so a later prompt in the
  ttl that something bears on is still briefed.
- `said_of` turns a brief made into what the row says: the rendered brief, nothing for an empty
  one, and for a failure `The brief was not made:`, the reason and the command
  `homma what --brief -`.

## CHANGE: what a brief keeps FROM any substring and every placed line TO whole lines, one reason, three quotes and a byte cap

`crates/homma-engine/src/cmd/what/brief.rs`

- `whole` takes a quote only where it is a whole line of the lines named, or all of them, by
  `bare`: whitespace folded, leading `#`, `-`, `*`, `+` and `>` and the `**` and `__` marks set
  aside on both sides.
- `Thing::why` is one `Option<String>`, set only from a line placed at the thing's heading;
  a second such line, and any placed line under the heading without a quote, is dropped.
- `QUOTES = 3` quotes a thing, the rest dropped. `BYTES = 3000`: `fit` leaves out things from
  the last while the rendered brief is over, counting them in `Brief::left`, then the one thing's
  quotes from its last, then cuts its reason and the note at a character boundary.
- The render says how many things were left out and that `homma what <words>` lists them, and
  what the dropped lines can have been.

## CHANGE: the tests of the brief and its row

`crates/homma-engine/src/cmd/what/brief_tests.rs`, `crates/homma-engine/src/cmd/nudge/tests/brief.rs`

- A second, different prompt inside the ttl is not briefed, one after it is; an empty brief does
  not count as said; the failure line names the command.
- A fragment is dropped and a whole line without its heading mark is taken; the whole range
  quote is taken; one reason from the heading, a body paraphrase and a second reason dropped;
  a fourth quote dropped; a brief over the cap keeps the best things and counts the rest; one
  thing over the cap alone is cut to fit.
- Each fails with its fix taken out: the ttl override back, a substring match, `QUOTES` unbounded,
  `fit` the identity, the body-line drop removed.

## CHANGE: the brief's printing and the row's text FROM their parents TO modules of their own

`crates/homma-engine/src/cmd/what/brief_render.rs`, `crates/homma-engine/src/cmd/nudge/brief.rs`

- `fit`, `shorten`, `Brief::render` and its human rendering move whole from `what/brief.rs` into
  `what/brief_render.rs`, and `said_of` from `nudge/mod.rs` into `nudge/brief.rs`, so neither
  parent passes 500 lines.
