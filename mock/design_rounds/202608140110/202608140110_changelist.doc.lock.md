# Changelist: doc — adopting mockspace

**Round:** 202608140100 adopting mockspace
**Phase:** DOC
**Scope:** the document templates the generator reads

## CHANGE: add the three workspace-level templates

`mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, `mock/PRINCIPLES.md.tmpl`, `mock/WORKFLOW.md.tmpl`.

homma has a `README.md` written for a reader arriving at the repository and no
design surface at all. The three templates are what mockspace renders the `docs/`
tree from, and a repository without them generates nothing.

Their content is deliberately thin in this round. DESIGN describes what homma is
and what its two crates currently hold, which the taxonomy round will replace.
PRINCIPLES carries the workspace's standing discipline as it applies here.
WORKFLOW carries the round flow and the responsible-tooling notice.

Writing them thick now would be writing a design the next round exists to settle.

## CHANGE: no per-crate templates yet

`mock/crates/*/DESIGN.md.tmpl` is where a crate states what it owns. Both existing
crates are about to be re-examined, so their per-crate documents are the taxonomy
round's output rather than this round's.
