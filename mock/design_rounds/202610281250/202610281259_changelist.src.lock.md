# src changelist

Answers the topic filed in this round, `homma-what-answers-how-to-questions-from-the-tools-readmes`, and builds the section "Finding a tool" of `mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl` as the doc changelist `202610281250_changelist.doc.lock.md` leaves it.

## Changes

- `sources/tools.rs`: the `README.md` beside a tool's descriptor is read when it is a plain file (a link is not followed). A line in a fenced block that begins with the tool's name or one of its commands' names is a usage line and becomes a verb whose name is the words after the command, whose summary is the text after a gap of two spaces, and whose command is the line without it with the tool's name replaced by `homma <name>` (or `homma tools run <name>`). A line that adds no words after the command, and one whose command a declared command already gave, adds nothing. The prose outside the fences is the tool's body. At most eighty usage lines and twenty-four thousand characters of prose are kept from one readme.
- `doc.rs`, `rank.rs`: a verb read from a usage line is marked an example. Its name carries arguments (`kaski status done`), so its words are weighed as a verb summary is and not as a verb name, and they are left out of the words two things are compared by, since a name that carries a worked invocation says what the verb does and not what it is called.
- `readme_tests.rs`: the usage line as a verb with its summary and command, marked an example, and weighed below a declared verb name; prose and another program's line not read; the question about reading an earlier message finding the `show` form; no readme, a bare line, an unclosed fence and a readme that is a link; the prose matching a question.
- `accept_tests.rs`: the question that went unanswered, asked of the real workspace, finds the `msg` tool and the `show` form.
