# Every hook refusal is reported, not thrown

**Date:** 2026-09-03
**Phase:** TOPIC
**Scope:** `homma-engine/src/cmd/release/mod.rs`, `homma-core/src/release/hook.rs`, one paragraph of `homma-core/DEEPDIVE_release.md.tmpl`
**Source topics:** the second review of pull request 49, and `202609030600/202609030600_topic.a-hook-that-cannot-be-read-is-still-a-hook.md`

## The findings

The engine reports the mockspace refusal as a line and a non-zero exit and throws the two newer ones as errors, so a sweep across the workspace dies on the first tracked hooks directory. The design groups the three as one kind; all three are reported the same way.

The tracked-directory check has no negative control: every test repo has no tracked file, so an over-broad pathspec refusing every real repo would fail no test. A repo with a commit and an untracked hooks directory asserts the write.

The design's paragraph on the refusals reads as three fragments borrowing a verb from the lead-in, and carries the one capitalised `Homma` in the document. Three full sentences, lowercase.

## What does not change

The refusals themselves.
