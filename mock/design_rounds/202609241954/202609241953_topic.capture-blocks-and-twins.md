# capture-blocks-and-twins

The second review of `homma capture` found three places where the design is
silent or narrower than the transcripts are, each checked against the real
files before this was written.

## A queued message can be blocks

A message typed during a turn with an image pasted in reaches the transcript as
a queued-command attachment whose `prompt` is a list of content blocks rather
than a string. Counted over every transcript on this machine: twelve, all from
the person, in shapes `image+text`, `text+image`, five images and a text, and
one image alone. Today one of them refuses the whole transcript, so a session
with one pasted screenshot cannot be captured at all.

A typed line with an image is already read as its text blocks joined by a blank
line, and the design never says so. Both get the same rule, said once: content
that is blocks is read as its text blocks, in order, joined by a blank line, and
blocks with no text in them are no words. An image alone is nothing said.

## A queued message can be written as two attachments

The first fix read an attachment and a typed line sharing a `source_uuid` once.
The harness also writes a queued message as two attachments with different
`uuid`s and the same `source_uuid`, and no typed line. In this workspace that is
two messages, four lines, and each is quoted twice today. So the rule is wider:
every human line naming or being a `source_uuid` is one message, read where the
first of them was written.

## A single-select answer is one label or text

Every question carries `multiSelect`. The labels-first split applies only where
it is true; on a single-select question an answer equal to a label is that
choice and anything else is what the person typed, whole, even where it starts
with a label. `Yes, but only after the release` against `Yes`/`No` is his
sentence.

## Smaller

- The `--since` help still says the later of it and the watermark wins; both
  apply, on different axes.
- The smoke test's check of the real path asserts nothing where the temporary
  directory is not reached through a link. It makes its own link instead.
- The newest-transcript test relies on two writes 20ms apart; it sets the
  modification times instead.
- `a_replayed_line_is_read_once` repeats what two later tests check, and goes.

## Not in this round

The voice tool reads a remark's context from a `Context:` line under the quote,
and a capture puts the agent's text in a fence above it, so remarks taken from
captures carry no context. That is a question of what the voice tool reads, not
of what capture writes, and it is left for the voice tool.
