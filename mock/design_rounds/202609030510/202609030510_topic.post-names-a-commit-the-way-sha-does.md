## The findings

`--post` matched the string it was given against the recorded runs, which carry a full sha, so a short one found nothing and said so, twelve lines from where `--sha` had just learnt to resolve what it is given. Both resolve the same way.

The status line is cut at a byte count, which panics off a character boundary; rustdoc's output is ascii so nothing reaches it today, and it is cut at a character boundary anyway.
