# A workspace's own words are synonym groups in its manifest

## The ask

The synonym table of `homma what` carries one workspace's vocabulary beside the tooling's: `msg` in the message group is the name of that workspace's messenger, and the group `helper`, `helpers`, `session`, `sessions`, `worker`, `wrapper` is how that workspace speaks of its sessions. homma names no workspace, so those words leave the table, and a workspace that speaks that way has to be able to say so.

## Decision

The compiled table keeps only groups of the tooling's own English: `msg` leaves the message group, and the session group leaves whole, since no word of it means another outside one workspace's arrangement. A workspace adds its own groups under `[what] synonyms`, an array of arrays of words, in its manifest. Its groups and the table's are one vocabulary: groups that share a word once stemmed are one group, so `["message", "msg"]` joins `msg` to the table's message group, and a group of words the table lacks is a group of its own.

Options weighed:
- Drop the two and add nothing: the workspace loses matches its people rely on, which the ticket names as the cost.
- Keep them compiled in with a neutral reading: `msg` reads neutral, but the session group does not, and the rule is that homma names none of a workspace's words.
- Its own groups in its manifest (taken): the vocabulary is the workspace's, as the design already says the catalogue its people's queries are tested on is.

## Reading the table

`[what]` is a table of homma's api, `WhatConfig`, refusing an unknown key, and the core holds it raw as it holds `[search]`, so a table that does not read stops only `homma what`, and stops it softly: the answer uses the compiled table alone and the footer says the workspace's groups were not read and why. The verb reads the manifest for nothing else, and with no manifest at all it answers from the compiled table as it does now.

## The order

`[what]` is a key an older engine refuses, so a manifest sets it only once every engine reading it lists `what.synonyms` among the keys it reads, which `homma status` shows. Nothing in the compiled table moves for a workspace before it sets the key, except the two removals; a workspace that relied on them sets the key once its engines read it.

## The removals wait: two landings, as the live defaults did

The order above removes the two from the table in the same landing that adds the key, and that moves something for a running workspace: its queries lose `msg` and the session words between the engine updating and its manifest setting the key, and its manifest cannot set the key until every engine reading it lists it. So:

1. This round: `[what] synonyms` is read and joined to the table; the table is unchanged, its two entries marked as the workspace's to move, citing the ticket. Nothing moves.
2. The workspace sets its groups once every engine reading its manifest lists `what.synonyms`.
3. A later round, on word that step 2 is done: `msg` and the session group leave the compiled table.
