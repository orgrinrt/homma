# Topic: a Hand reasons from what it was told, not from what the system does

**Date:** 2026-08-14
**Phase:** TOPIC
**Scope:** the third instance of one class, and the rule it implies
**Status:** settled

Three defects were found by standing a Hand up and asking it questions. All three
have the same shape, and the shape is worth more than any of the fixes.

**One.** Asked its git identity, it answered with the machine's global one. Every
commit it made was correctly authored, because the identity is set in that
workspace's git config.

**Two.** It committed to a trunk. The workspace forbids this everywhere.

**Three.** Asked whether its twin may write memory, it said yes. The twin's
generated definition carries no memory key, so the twin structurally cannot.

In every case **the mechanism was correct and the Hand's belief was wrong**, and
in every case the reason is the same: the generated definition did not say so.

## The rule

**A generated definition states every constraint the system enforces on the
participant it describes.** Not because the constraint needs restating to work,
but because a Hand answers questions about itself from what it was told, and a
Hand that answers wrongly about its own constraints will eventually act on that
answer. The third case is the clearest: it would have told a peer that its twin
could record something, and the peer would have believed it.

The general form, which is the one to carry forward: **a mechanism nobody is told
about is a mechanism that works and misleads.** Enforcement and explanation are
different jobs and the system owes both.

## What this round does

The prime's definition now states what its twin can and cannot do. A test pins
it, as with the previous two.

## What it does not do, and should be watched

There is no mechanical guarantee that the generated text and the enforced
behaviour stay in step. Three constraints are stated because three were found
wrong by hand; a fourth added to the mechanism without being added to the text
would reintroduce exactly this. A test asserting that every enforced constraint
appears in the generated body would need a list of constraints to check against,
and that list does not exist yet. Named here rather than left implicit.
