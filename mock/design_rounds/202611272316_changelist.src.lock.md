## CHANGE: the engine's manifest FROM untested at its renki requirements TO held to the exact requirement by a test and its control
File: `mock/crates/homma-engine/tests/one_renki.rs`
Verification: `the_engine_takes_its_renki_crates_exactly` reads `mock/crates/homma-engine/Cargo.toml` and fails on a `renki*` requirement that does not start with `=`, and failed on the manifest with `renki = "0.0.5-rc.4"` and `renki-dirs = "0.0.2"` under `cfg(unix)` before it was green on `=0.0.5-rc.4` and `=0.0.2`; `the_engine_check_flags_a_caret_requirement_under_the_unix_target` is its control, and the test also asserts that the manifest names renki at all, so an emptied manifest cannot pass.

## CHANGE: the exact-requirement predicate FROM one in the launcher's own test TO one file both tests read
File: `launcher/tests/support/renki_requirements.rs`, `launcher/tests/a_publishing_crate_is_publishable.rs`
Verification: `loose_renki` and `names_renki` live in the support file, which is no test target; the launcher's `this_launcher_takes_its_renki_crates_exactly` and the engine's test both call them from it, the engine's by the path `../../../../launcher/tests/support/renki_requirements.rs`, so no second copy of the predicate exists; `the_check_tells_an_exact_renki_from_a_caret_one` is the launcher's control and covers the caret, bare, path-only and target-table shapes.
