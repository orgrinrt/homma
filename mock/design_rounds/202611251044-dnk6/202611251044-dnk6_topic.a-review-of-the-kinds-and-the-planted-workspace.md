# a-review-of-the-kinds-and-the-planted-workspace

Follows the closed rounds 202611251020-u09e and 202611251034-l741. A review found:

## The planted workspace is not neutral

Its scripts, their headers and its tools were close copies of one real workspace's. Decision: every
name, header and summary in it is invented and generic, and the design's relatedness paragraph
stops naming a real tool.

## A principle and a rule writing one card

A principle `x` and a rule `principle-x` both write `principle-x.md`, the last one winning in
silence. Decision: the load of the principles refuses a principle whose card name a rule has, as a
mode whose skill name an authored skill has is refused.

## The principle's title

The title check took the first `# ` line, so a card opening with `## Foo` passed. Decision: the
first heading of any level must be `# <title prefix><name>`.

## The cards' writing

`render_cards` wrote with its own newline handling. Decision: it writes through `surface::write`.

## A test that lost half

The acceptance test of a tool with verbs and no default lost its check that no tool hit prints a
bare command. Decision: it asks a query the planted tools answer and checks the hits too.

## Left for follow-ups

The personas' corpus onto the one path, and engine-level tests of `skills render` (the clash
refusal, the gate's removal, mode skills kept out of the unclaimed list), of `rules render` and
`rules load` with principles, and of `ask` sweeping on its run, are tickets.
