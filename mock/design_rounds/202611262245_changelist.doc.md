## CHANGE: `homma what` FROM words only TO words or a work's text with `--brief`

`mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, new subsection `### A brief of what bears on a unit of work`
under `## Finding a tool`.

- `homma what --brief <file|->` ranks the sources by the work's text with a floor
  of two words in place of the query's share, reads no mockspace command, and
  hands the candidates' own text to the summarising pass under a brief asking
  for the things that bear on the work and their lines quoted exactly.
- Every quoted span is checked against the content lines its place names; a line
  that fails is dropped and counted. The brief prints by thing: kind, name,
  command, why, quoted lines.
- Off or failed pass: the candidates as a query prints them, and the reason.
- Kept by the ask's cache under `homma/brief/`, keyed on the work's text, both
  rules, the command, the model and each candidate's digest; each brief is a line
  of `homma/brief.log`.

## CHANGE: a nudge row FROM a fixed text TO a fixed text or a brief

`mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, section `## A nudge`.

- The text `matches` reads is named: a shell command, a tool input as JSON, and
  for an event with no tool the payload's `prompt`.
- `brief = true` on an `inform` row says the brief of that text after `says`,
  due once per text in a session and again after `ttl`; an empty text or an
  empty brief says nothing; on any other strength the manifest is refused.
- The record keeps, for a brief row, the digest of the text it was said for.
