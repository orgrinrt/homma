## Verified

`cargo test -p homma-engine` on the mac: every suite green, 253 in the binary's
own tests, 45 of them under `cmd::capture`, and 4 in `capture_smoke`. The
negative control: with the fence's two-space indent taken out, the three tests
asserting it fail (`an_agent_line_opening_with_a_quote_mark_would_be_his_at_the_margin`,
`only_the_persons_words_are_blockquotes`,
`the_same_context_is_not_written_twice_in_a_row`) and nothing else does. Run
against this workspace's own live transcript with a floor, it wrote one capture
of four messages and one answered round, the harness's own lines absent, and a
second run wrote nothing. `cargo clippy -p homma-engine --all-targets` reports
nothing in the new module.
