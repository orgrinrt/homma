## CHANGE: an entry one edit from a known name FROM taken as unread TO refused as a misspelling
File: `mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl`
Verification: The section says an entry one edit away from a name this engine reads in the same table is refused as a misspelling. One edit is one character inserted, removed or replaced, or two neighbours swapped. The section names `[defualts]`, `homma_brnach` and `[agent.nudge]`.

## CHANGE: a refusal after the unread entries are taken out FROM losing its line TO keeping it
File: `mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl`
Verification: The section says the second parse reads the manifest's own text with each unread entry's lines blanked, so a refusal of what is left keeps its line. An entry the line reading does not follow is taken out of the parsed value instead.

## CHANGE: the words FROM said by status and the refusing regen alone TO also once on stderr by every other verb
File: `mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl`
Verification: The section says the words are said by `homma status` in its report, by `agent regen` when it refuses, and once on stderr by every other verb that loads the manifest. It no longer says that no other verb prints them.
