## CHANGE: `README.md`

The `deny` paragraph says what the list does not cover. The workspace root is
permitted after every entry is folded in, so an entry naming it does not stop
`agent regen` writing there, and the reason that permission exists gets its own
sentence rather than being left for a reader to hit.

The same paragraph says where `~/` works, since it expands in a `deny` entry
and not in `local_path` or `workspace.path`, and a reader who learns the tilde
in one block will try it in the next.
