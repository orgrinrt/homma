## Changes

- CHANGE `capture::store::escaped`: a spelling longer than `store::LONGEST`, two
  hundred units, is cut there and followed by `-` and `store::hashed` of the
  whole path. A test pins the harness's own suffixes for four paths, read under
  bun from the harness's functions: a negative and a positive hash, a path of
  many separators, and one with an emoji and non-ASCII letters, which only
  hashing over UTF-16 units gets right; and a path spelled to exactly two
  hundred units is kept whole. Two controls were run by hand and both went red
  on the arm they aim at: hashing over `char`s failed the emoji case, and `>=`
  for `>` failed the exact-limit case.
- CHANGE `capture::store::hashed`: new, the 32-bit string hash wrapped, its
  absolute value in base 36.
- CHANGE `capture::transcript::Event::before`: the `FIXME` says only what is
  missing, that the context is copied whole and nothing summarises it yet.
- The lines read out of the harness are
  `mock/research/202609242353_harness-project-directory/extracted.txt`.
- `docs/DESIGN.md` re-renders from the template through `cargo mock`.
