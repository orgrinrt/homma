## CHANGE: no per-crate document changes

No `mock/crates/*/DESIGN.md.tmpl` is edited by this round, and the reason is a
finding rather than a convenience.

The aggregation mechanism lives in the `homma` binary crate and its config
anchoring lives in `homma-core`. Neither crate ships a design document:
`mock/crates/` carries one for `homma-api`, `homma-org` and `homma-store` only.
So the contract this round changes has no document to be changed in.

The contract is stated in the topic instead, under "What the wrapper should key
on instead", and the gap is recorded there under "Out of scope". Writing the two
missing design documents is real work with its own scope and is not folded into
a fix.
