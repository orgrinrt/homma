## mock/crates/homma-engine

- `src/cmd/release/commands.rs`, new: the workspace's own commands a release runs. `workspace_command`
  reads one from `[paths]` of the manifest, absolute from the manifest's directory, an error where the
  manifest does not read or names one that is not there; `forge_route_of` moves here from `mod.rs` onto it,
  and `history_scan_of` is the second. `history_refusal(scan, root)` runs the scan with the clone's root as
  its one argument and returns `None` on exit 0, and otherwise the refusal: what the scan printed, whether the
  history was flagged (exit 1) or could not be scanned (any other exit, a kill, a scan that could not start),
  and the remedy, a rehouse onto a clean history.
- `src/cmd/release/mod.rs`: `run_cmd` reads `history_scan_of` beside `forge_route_of`, and for each repo it
  would release runs `history_refusal` before the forge, the registries or the plan are asked, with
  `--dry-run` too; a refusal fails the run with that repo's line and releases nothing after it.
- `src/cmd/release/commands_tests.rs`, new: a flagged history refused with what the scan said and the rehouse,
  a clean one passing, a scan that exits 2 or is not there refused, and the manifest read.
- `tests/cli_release_history.rs`, new: through the binary, a dry run of a repo whose history carries a
  `Co-Authored-By: Claude` trailer fails with the scan's words and moves nothing.
