# the-bounded-run-keeps-its-grace-and-ends-only-its-own-group

Follows the closed round `202611241541-y562`, which made the engine's one bounded run of a child.
A review of it found the run cutting the grace short, able to signal a group that is no longer the
child's, and taking an interrupted read for the end of a pipe.

## The question

What does the bounded run owe the pipes once the child exits, and which group may it signal?

## What is settled

- **The grace is a second, whatever is left of the limit.** The design gives the child its limit
  to exit and then a second for its pipes. The run took the smaller of the second and what was
  left of the limit, so a child exiting just under its limit lost the tail of what it wrote, and a
  call that printed its whole answer failed as one that printed none.
- **The child is not reaped before its group is ended.** Its exit is read without reaping it, so
  its number stays its own, and with it the number of its group, until the group is signalled; then
  it is reaped. A signal sent after the reaping could reach another group that took the number.
  Where the platform cannot read an exit without reaping, the run reaps first as before.
- **A process that left the group is not reached.** One the child started that made a session or
  group of its own is outside what ending the group ends. Its pipe is given up after the grace, and
  what arrived is all there is.
- **An interrupted read is read again.** A read the system interrupted is not the end of the pipe.
- **The reason keeps the limit's fraction.** A limit of a second and a half is said as such, not
  as a second.
- **The rules version's test is a pin.** It checks the version against the value the rules have
  now, so an edit of the rules fails it until the pin moves; its control compared another string's
  digest and could not fail, and is gone.
- **The engine asks `rustix` at 1.1.6 or later**, the first release past its advisory, so a
  resolve without the lockfile cannot pick one before it.
- The closed round's src changelist names `summary/parts.rs` among the files its tests touched;
  that round did not change it. Nothing in this round changes it either.
