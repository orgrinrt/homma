# src changelist

Descends from `202611241611-nl0h_topic.the-bounded-run-keeps-its-grace-and-ends-only-its-own-group.md` and the locked document changelist `202611241611-nl0h_changelist.doc.lock.md`. Each new test carries a control that fails where its mechanism is taken out.

## CHANGE: the bounded run FROM the grace cut to what is left of the limit, the child reaped before its group is signalled, and an interrupted read ending the pipe TO a whole second of grace, the child reaped after its group is ended where the platform can read an exit without reaping, and an interrupted read read again
File: `mock/crates/homma-engine/src/cmd/util/bounded.rs`, `mock/crates/homma-engine/src/cmd/util/bounded/tests.rs`
Verification: `run` waits `GRACE` after the exit whatever is left of the limit; on Linux, Android, macOS, FreeBSD and NetBSD the exit is read through `rustix::process::waitid` with `NOWAIT`, the group is signalled where a pipe stays open, and the child is reaped after; `drain` retries `ErrorKind::Interrupted`; tests: a child exiting with a tenth of a second of its limit left, whose output reaches its pipe only after the exit through a process it started, keeps that output whole (control: the same output written after the grace is lost); the pid-file tests wait for the pid file before reading it and the control on a closed pipe allows three seconds.

## CHANGE: the reason for a run past its limit FROM whole seconds TO the limit as given, with its fraction
File: `mock/crates/homma-engine/src/cmd/util/bounded.rs`, `mock/crates/homma-engine/src/cmd/util/bounded/tests.rs`
Verification: `Stopped::reason` says milliseconds under a second, whole seconds where the limit is whole, and seconds with their fraction otherwise; a test names 600 ms, 300 s and 1.5 s.

## CHANGE: the rules version's test FROM a pin and a control that cannot fail TO the pin alone
File: `mock/crates/homma-engine/src/cmd/summary/tests.rs`
Verification: `the_rules_version_is_pinned_to_the_rules_as_they_stand` asserts the pinned value and nothing else.

## CHANGE: the `rustix` requirement FROM any 1.x TO 1.1.6 or later
File: `mock/crates/homma-engine/Cargo.toml`
