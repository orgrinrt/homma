# a-listing-says-its-line-and-its-json-as-they-are

Follows the closed round 202611250648-ylo2, which added `homma search --fresh [<days>]`. A review of
it found the source saying three things the design did not, a window of no days reachable from the
code, and the two places homma holds what the index crate's layer should hold unmarked.

## The line of a place in a listing

The design says a place's line in a listing says the same as for a question. It says the same of
how the place was brought current, behind, or why it was not asked; but a question's line says the
place was asked by its words, and a listing asks no words. Decision: the line of a place listed reads
`listed by the days they changed` where a question's reads `asked by words`, the rest unchanged. The
design says so.

## The JSON of a listing

A listing has no question. Decision: its JSON leaves out `question`, as it is empty, beside the
`window` and the `age` it adds. The design says so.

## The window

A window of no days lists nothing and is no listing. The flag already refuses it; decision: the
days are a non-zero count in the types from the flag down, so `list` takes no zero and reports the
window it was given, and nothing turns one number into another.

## What is homma's only for now

Two things wait on the index crate's layer: the refusal of words beside `--fresh`, and the merge of
the indexes' lists by day. Each carries the repository's marker naming the ticket that moves it to
the crate.

## The README

The command table's `homma search` row says only that it asks the indexes as one list; it gains
the listing of what changed in the last days.

## Tests

With failing controls: a commit late on one day in UTC, listed on the next day after midnight, is
a day old and outside a window of one day; a repository index built without its history lists its
files and no commit; a place missing and a place shut keep their lines in a listing; `age` is in the
JSON; `--limit` bounds what the pass reads.
