# the-ask-says-what-it-prints-and-refuses-a-terminal

Follows the closed round `202611241631-bolh`, which took the command out of `homma ask`. A review of
it found the design still describing a command's exit status in the printed line, and the verb
waiting forever when nothing is piped to it.

## The question

What does the ask print after its answer, and what does it do with no input piped to it?

## What is settled

- **The last line says the chunks and the cache, and nothing of an exit status.** The ask runs no
  command, so it knows no status; the line says how many chunks of input there were and how many
  partials came from the cache, or that the whole answer did. A command's exit status belongs to
  the caller's pipeline, which sees it as it sees any.
- **A terminal on stdin is refused.** With nothing piped, stdin is the terminal and reading it to
  its end waits on a person who is not typing. The ask says nothing was piped to it and exits 1
  without reading.
- **A second slow accept test of `homma what` is ignored with its ticket**,
  `the_free_disk_accept_test_of_homma_what_runs_over_a_minute`.
