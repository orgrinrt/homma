# src changelist

Topic: `202611231638_topic.agent-hook-dispatchers.md`; the doc changelist is locked.

## mock/crates/homma-api

- `src/agent_hooks.rs`: `DEFAULT_TIMEOUT`, 60. `AgentHook` carries `timeout` and `async` (`background`), set
  through `with_timeout`, which refuses 0, and `in_background`, read through `timeout()` and `background()`; a
  row reads both. `AgentTable` carries `timeout` and `filter` beside `hooks`, built through `AgentTable::new`,
  which refuses a timeout of 0 and a filter whose first word is not a relative path inside the workspace;
  `timeout()` gives the table's or the default, `filter()` the words. `InvalidAgentHooks` gains `Timeout` and
  `Filter`. Tests for each, with their controls.
- `src/lib.rs`: re-exports `DEFAULT_TIMEOUT`.

## mock/crates/homma-engine

- `src/cmd/dispatch.rs` (new): `Entry`, the table `_dispatch.json` with its mark, `previous` reading it back,
  `install` writing the table and the dispatcher `_dispatch.sh` and removing every marked wrapper but the
  dispatcher and the gate, `registrations` (one per event and mode, matchers joined or every tool, the longest
  timeout plus ten seconds, `async` for the background ones), `is_dispatcher_command`, and `dispatcher_script`,
  the bash the design describes: the filter once round it, one `jq` over the payload and the table, the host's
  reading of a matcher, the narrowing by `LANDS_IN_SH`, each hook run side by side under `timeout`, and the fold.
- `src/cmd/dispatch_tests.rs`, `dispatch_scope_tests.rs`, `dispatch_fold_tests.rs` (new): the registrations, the
  table, the refusals, the retirement of the wrappers, and the dispatcher run as bash: the event and mode, the
  matchers, the program and arguments, side by side, the timeout, the filter, no `jq`; the narrowing cases the
  wrapper tests held, ported; and the fold: deny over ask over allow, every context, exit 2 carrying the others'
  context, block, continue and messages, plain text, another failure, one answer passed through.
- `src/cmd/aggregate.rs`: `aggregate_repo` takes the default timeout and makes `Entry`s, narrowed to the repo,
  with the timeout and `async` each registration gives; no wrapper is written. `HookEntry` carries `timeout` and
  `background` and writes them. The rule sweep keeps its own `clean_stale`; the hook sweep moves to `install`.
- `src/cmd/aggregate_wrapper.rs`: `wrapper_script` is removed; `LANDS_IN_SH` reads `FILE`, `CWD` and `CMD` the
  dispatcher has set rather than reading them with three `jq`s.
- `src/cmd/aggregate_settings.rs`: `merge_settings(root, known_repos, registrations)` writes the dispatcher's
  registrations and sweeps, in both files alike, every registration of homma's: a retired shape, the dispatcher,
  or a `<x>--<y>` name whose file is absent or marked.
- `src/cmd/declared.rs`: `declared_entries` makes an `Entry` of each row, with its timeout or `[agent] timeout`,
  its `async`, its repositories made relative; `install_declared`, `declared_wrapper`, `is_declared_command` and
  `DECLARED_PREFIX` are removed.
- `src/cmd/gates.rs`: `install_workspace_gate` takes the timeout and returns the gate's `Entry`;
  `GATE_SCRIPT_NAME` is `pub(crate)`; `is_workspace_gate_command` is removed.
- `src/cmd/agent.rs`: the regen gathers entries, keeps those of the members it did not visit that the manifest
  still names from the last table, puts the gate's and the rows' first, writes the dispatcher and merges its
  registrations.
- `src/cmd/mod.rs`: `pub mod dispatch`.
- Tests ported to the entries and the dispatcher: `aggregate_tests.rs` (and a hook keeping its timeout and
  `async`), `aggregate_chains_tests.rs`, `aggregate_registration_tests.rs`, `aggregate_settings_tests.rs`,
  `aggregate_settings_shared_tests.rs`, `declared_tests.rs` (and a row's timeout, `async` and the table's
  default), `gates/tests.rs`. `aggregate_wrapper_tests.rs` and `aggregate_wrapper_handoff_tests.rs` are removed,
  their cases in `dispatch_scope_tests.rs`. `tests/regen_dispatch_smoke.rs` (new): one registration per event, its
  timeout, a run over one member keeping the other's entries, a member gone taking its own.
