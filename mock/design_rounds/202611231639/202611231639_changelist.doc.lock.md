## mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl

The agent hooks:

- CHANGE: the paragraph saying one registration per hook is what homma writes, and that a dispatcher is not
  needed, is replaced. homma writes one dispatcher, `.claude/hooks/_dispatch.sh`, and the table it reads,
  `.claude/hooks/_dispatch.json`, and registers the dispatcher at most twice per event, once for the hooks the
  call waits on and once for the `async` ones. Two new paragraphs say how a call is matched, narrowed, run side by
  side under each hook's timeout, and how the answers fold: exit 2 wins and carries the others'
  `additionalContext`; otherwise the host's precedence, every context and message kept; a plain-text answer kept
  as text, or folded into context on `SessionStart` and `UserPromptSubmit`; another failure named on stderr; no
  `jq` is exit 1 with a line. The handler's matcher is the entries' joined, its timeout the longest plus ten
  seconds, and `if` is not used, with why.
- CHANGE: a member's hook is an entry rather than a registration, and keeps the timeout and `async` its
  repository's registration gives it, narrowed to its own repository.
- CHANGE: `[agent.hooks]` rows take `timeout` and `async`, with an example of each and when a hook is `async`.
  `[agent]` takes `timeout` (default 60) and `filter`, a program as a list of words with its path under the
  workspace first, which the dispatcher runs itself through. It replaces the paragraph saying every wrapper runs
  through `tools/hookdedup/hookdedup run` with no key for it.
- CHANGE: the narrowing paragraphs say the dispatcher where they said the wrapper; what they describe is the
  same.
- CHANGE: a run over some members keeps the entries of those it did not visit, read back from the table, and
  drops those of a member the manifest no longer names.
- CHANGE: the wrappers homma wrote before, `<repo>--<name>.sh` and `_declared--<event>--<run>.sh`, are removed with
  their registrations on the next regen; the workspace gate keeps its file and is an entry.
- CHANGE: the `agent_surfaces` paragraph says the regen for the agent hooks, not for the hook wrappers.
