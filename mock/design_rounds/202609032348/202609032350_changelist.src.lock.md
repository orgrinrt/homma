# Source changelist: the poster knows its workspace and records giving up

**Date:** 2026-09-03
**Phase:** IMPL
**Doc changelist:** `202609032348_changelist.doc.lock.md`

## `homma-api`

### `src/poster.rs` (add)

CHANGE: `PosterGaveUp` (add), the `poster-gave-up` record kind with `repo`,
`sha`, `gave_up_at` and `waited_secs`, round-tripping through `Record`.
Verification: `a_give_up_survives_the_store_and_the_count_comes_back_as_a_number`,
`a_gate_run_record_is_not_a_give_up_and_a_bad_count_is_refused` and
`two_give_ups_on_one_commit_are_two_records` in the same file.

### `src/lib.rs` (modify)

CHANGE: `poster` module declared and its three names re-exported.

## `homma-engine`

### `src/cmd/release/gating.rs` (modify)

CHANGE: `poster_args(manifest, name, sha)` FROM `release gate <name> --post
<sha> --await` TO the same behind `--config <manifest>`, the manifest made
absolute by `absolute(path)` (add) from the hook's resolved config path.
Verification: `the_poster_parses_back_as_a_waiting_post_on_the_manifest_it_was_handed`
parses the argv through `Cli::try_parse_from` and asserts the config, the
repository, the sha and the wait; it is red on the previous revision, which
named no config. `a_relative_manifest_is_made_absolute_and_an_absolute_one_is_kept`.

CHANGE: the posters are left after the loop over the tips, and only when
every tip is green, FROM one poster left per green tip inside the loop.
Verification: read `gate_cmd`; `runs` is collected in the loop and walked
under `if hook && ok`.

CHANGE: `await_known` pauses after every miss FROM skipping the pause after
the last, and `TRIES` (add) is `AWAIT / PAUSE`.
Verification: `the_bound_is_the_ten_minutes_every_document_names` holds
`AWAIT` to 600 seconds, `PAUSE * TRIES` to `AWAIT`, and times three misses
at three pauses.

CHANGE: a poster that gives up appends a `PosterGaveUp` to the store before
reporting, FROM saying so on a stdout that is `/dev/null`.
Verification: read the `--await` arm of `gate_cmd`; the record type's own
tests cover what is written.

## `homma-core`

### `src/forge/github.rs` (modify)

CHANGE: `commit_known` FROM reading 404 and 422 as the commit unknown TO
reading 422 as that and 404 as `RepoNotFound` through `map_ureq_error`.
Verification: `a_sha_github_has_not_received_is_unknown_and_a_repo_it_has_not_is_an_error`
in `github_wire_tests.rs`, against a stub answering by path.

### `src/forge/forgejo.rs` (modify)

CHANGE: `commit_known` FROM reading 404 and 422 as the commit unknown TO
reading 404 as the commit unknown when `repo_exists` answers yes and as
`RepoNotFound` when it answers no.
Verification: `a_forgejo_404_is_the_commit_when_the_repo_answers_and_the_repo_when_it_does_not`
in `forgejo_wire_tests.rs`.

### `src/forge/trait_def.rs` (modify)

CHANGE: the `commit_known` doc names both paths, the status each forge
answers for a sha it lacks, and the repository-level 404 as an error.

### `src/forge/github_wire_tests.rs`, `src/forge/forgejo_wire_tests.rs` (modify)

CHANGE: `status_by_path` (add), a loopback stub answering each request by
the first matching route, 404 otherwise, for the tests above.
