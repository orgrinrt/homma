## CHANGE: `Command::Search` FROM words required and a limit of ten TO words required unless `--fresh [<days>]`, which refuses them, and a limit that is ten only for a question
File: `mock/crates/homma-engine/src/cli.rs`
Verification: `fresh` is `Option<u32>`, three where the flag is given bare, at least one, conflicting with `words`; `limit` is `Option<u32>`; `fresh_parses_bare_with_a_number_and_refuses_words` holds all three.

## CHANGE: the search's dispatch FROM the pass only on `--summarise` TO the pass on `--summarise`, or with `--fresh` wherever `[summary]` is on
File: `mock/crates/homma-engine/src/cmd/mod.rs`
Verification: `search::Asked` is built from `fresh` and `words`; the summary settings are read where either asks and dropped for a bare `--fresh` where the pass is off.

## CHANGE: the search FROM one question TO a question or a listing, through the same opening of each place and the same line per index
File: `mock/crates/homma-engine/src/cmd/search/mod.rs`
Verification: `Asked` names the two and `run` takes it with an optional limit, `QUESTION_LIMIT` where a question gives none; `open_current` and `index_out` are the one opening and the one line both use, and `summarised` the one way to the pass; `SearchOut` carries `window` and `HitOut` carries `age`; `brief` and the empty line say the listing's own words; `ask` and `answer` keep their signatures.

## CHANGE: FROM nothing TO the listing of what changed in a window
File: `mock/crates/homma-engine/src/cmd/search/fresh.rs`
Verification: `answer` is `list` through `summarised`; `list` opens each place through `open_current`, lists a repository index with `RepoIndex::newest` over `filter::Changed::within`, says a corpus keeps no days, merges by day, then place order, then the index's own order, and bounds by the limit.

## CHANGE: FROM nothing TO tests of the listing over planted repositories with dated commits
File: `mock/crates/homma-engine/src/cmd/search/tests_fresh.rs`
Verification: each test has its failing control: a file changed outside the window not listed, a commit of the window listed under `.git/`, a corpus not asked and said so, the order newest first across two places, the limit, the age words, and the pass run without `--summarise` and silent where off.
