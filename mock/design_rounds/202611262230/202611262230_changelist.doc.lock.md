## CHANGE: the answer of a `once` row FROM any call carrying the marker TO two exact shapes

`mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, section `## A nudge`, the answer paragraph.

- Was: a call whose command is the stopped one with the marker added passes; any
  other call carrying the marker is the answer and runs as written.
- Now: the marker counts only as `<the stopped command> # <marker> <why>` with the
  comment on the last line and nothing after it, or as `: <marker> <why>` alone on
  one line with no `;`, `&`, `|`, `<`, `>`, `$(` or backtick. A marker in any
  other shape answers nothing, and the call meets the rows as written.
- Why: a second command after the marker, or a different one before it, ran
  without any row looking at it.
