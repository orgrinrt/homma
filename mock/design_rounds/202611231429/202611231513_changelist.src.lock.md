# src changelist

Topic: `202611231428_topic.identity-catalogue-and-lease.md`; the doc changelist is locked again after review pass
one reopened it.

## Comparison to deprecated changelist

`202611231431_changelist.src.deprecated.md` was locked and is superseded by this one, which claims everything it
claimed and adds review pass one's fixes. Every bullet below that the deprecated changelist also carries is
unchanged in what it claims; the additions are marked "Added".

## mock/crates/homma-api

- `src/catalogue.rs`, new: `Office` (`holder`, `counted` true by default) and `LeaseConfig` (`remote`, `branch`,
  `minutes`; `origin`, `main`, 120), with their parse tests.
- `src/config.rs`: `Role::Journeyman`; `Identity::aliases`, classified free-form in the control-character tests;
  `Paths::identities` (`.shared/.index/identities.tsv`) and `Paths::leases` (`.shared/state/leases`);
  `Workspace::offices` and `Workspace::lease`.
- `src/reference.rs`: a journeyman's record stands as agent output, as every other agent role's.
- `src/lib.rs`: `catalogue` exported, `LeaseConfig` and `Office` re-exported.

## mock/crates/homma-org

- `src/catalogue.rs`, new, tests in `src/catalogue/tests.rs`: `normalise` (trimmed, lowercased, the plus tag of the
  local part stripped), `handle_fault` (the handle checked as a name, a separator named before a leading dot),
  `CatalogueFault`, `check_catalogue` (names, aliases and addresses used once, office holders present and holding
  one office each, no office named like a participant), `is_agent`. Added: `resolve`, a handle or an alias to its
  entry; an office's holder is resolved through it, and two offices are counted against the resolved handle.
- `src/lease.rs`, new, tests in `src/lease/tests.rs`: `Lease` with `parse`, `render` and `is_live`; `Ask`,
  `Outcome`, `Refusal` and `decide`, the rule the design states.
- `src/lib.rs`: `OrgRegistry::agent_at` and `OrgRegistry::agent_addresses`, with their tests; the two modules
  declared, `check_catalogue` and `CatalogueFault` re-exported. Added: the committer test matches a distinct
  committer address and lists a repeated one once.
- `Cargo.toml`: `jiff` with `serde`, and `toml`, for the lease record.

## mock/crates/homma-core

- `src/swap.rs`, new, tests in `src/swap/tests.rs` over a bare remote and two clones: `Swapped` and `swap_file`,
  the tree built in a scratch index under the git directory, the commit on the read tip, a push without force, a
  refusal as not a fast-forward answered `Moved`, the tracking ref moved on a landing. Added: a failure to move the
  tracking ref after a landing is not the swap's failure.
- `src/config.rs`: `offices` and `lease` carried as values, so the deny-unknown parser accepts the file the
  registry parser reads.
- `src/lib.rs`: `swap` declared.

## mock/crates/homma-engine

- `src/cli.rs`: `IdentityOp::Match`, `IdentityOp::Index`, `IdentityOp::Lease` with `LeaseOp` (`take`, `renew`,
  `release`, `show`); `RoleArg::Journeyman`; the `org add` handle help says what a handle may carry.
- `src/cmd/lease.rs`, new, tests in `src/cmd/lease/tests.rs` over a bare remote and two clones: `record_path`,
  `read`, `Done`, `run` (read, decide, swap, again on a moved trunk, `TRIES` times) and `describe`. Added: `entry`
  (a name resolved through its aliases, refused when the catalogue lacks it or it is the king's), `MINUTES` (one
  minute to a week, given or configured), and `read` refusing a record that is there and cannot be read or names
  another handle; tests for each.
- `src/cmd/identity.rs`: `index_text`, `role_word`, `write_index` and `match_line`, with tests.
- `src/cmd/org.rs`: `load` refuses every fault `check_catalogue` finds, and `add` refuses one before it writes;
  `check_handle` asks `handle_fault`; tests for a handle with a dot or an umlaut, an address another entry
  answers to refused on add, a broken catalogue refused whole, and offices read.
- `tests/derived_paths_smoke.rs`, `tests/containment_smoke.rs`: the fixture hands each get an address of their
  own, since two hands sharing one is now a broken catalogue.
- `src/cmd/mod.rs`: the three identity verbs dispatched; `agent regen` writes the index before its stages, saying
  and going on when the catalogue does not load. Added: an empty index where no agent is catalogued; `lease show`
  and the printed line name the resolved handle.
- `tests/catalogue_smoke.rs`, new: `match` through a plus tag, a person exiting 1, `index` and `regen` writing the
  index, a broken catalogue refused naming the address and the entry.
