# Topic: the store question goes to op, with two readings

**Date:** 2026-08-14
**Phase:** TOPIC
**Scope:** correcting a call made on one expert's word
**Status:** open, and op's

The settled-taxonomy topic asserted that correspondence and structural records
share one storage engine, on one expert's reading. A second independent read
disagreed. **No call about what the design permits is made on one expert's word**,
so this is withdrawn to an open question and the two readings are recorded.

## The first reading: one engine

A message is a record whose kind is `message`; a channel is a namespace; the
append-only property is a flag a kind declares. What it buys: tasks, epochs,
cursors and later monitoring records are one code path; the relation graph runs
over one store; the single-writer property covers everything at once rather than
being re-argued when tasks arrive.

## The second reading: two engines, one vocabulary

Append-only is not a spare bit. It is the reason the hub, the daemon and the
single-writer story exist at all, and a task has none of that problem: one Hand
mutates its status and there is no documented concurrent-writer race. A kind flag
that forks the entire write path is two engines sharing a door.

It also cites two things op already decided. He cut immutability back to
correspondence alone when an earlier draft generalised it. And he rejected pure
event sourcing outright, in the same stretch of rounds; reusing an append-and-fold
shape for tasks is event sourcing for tasks.

## Where they agree, which is what tonight uses

Both readings agree that three things are shared regardless of how many engines
there are, and that building them twice is the duplication to avoid:

- **Provenance.** A reference whose rung is derived from what it points at. It
  does not care what wrote the record.
- **Relation extraction.** A pure function over text producing graph edges.
- **Date typing.** The agenda queries the type, never a field name.

And both agree the build order already defers the question: correspondence is
built before structural records exist, and stage three looks identical either way.

## What this round therefore does

Writes those three as **storage-agnostic pure functions** from the first line,
rather than baked into whatever reads the correspondence files. That costs nothing
tonight, because they are pure functions under either reading, and it is what
makes the later work cheap under both.

**The physical question stays open and is op's**, with both readings above.
