# Doc changelist: a tool's paste with line breaks round it

Topic: `202609250835_topic.a-tool-paste-with-blank-lines-round-it.md`.

## Current state

`mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl` says a human line is a tool's when its words are a
recorded `text`, bare or inside one `<pasted_content id="…">` wrapper. The
harness also puts line breaks outside the wrapper, which that sentence does not
allow for, so a tool's paste arriving that way is quoted as the person's.

## Changes proposed

`mock/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, the typed-by-tools paragraph: the wrapper case now
allows nothing outside the wrapper but line breaks. Every other condition stays
as written: the text inside the wrapper is the whole recorded text, the wrapper
is one and not nested, a bare text still has to be the words exactly, and the
sixty-second bound and the one line per record are unchanged.
