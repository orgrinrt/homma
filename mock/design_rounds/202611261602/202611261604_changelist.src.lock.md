Verification: `make_executable` is gone; the hook, wrapper and dedup stand-ins, the chains and declared hooks, the dispatch stubs, the planted `what` workspace and the scripts of the sources tests each go through `crate::cmd::planted::script` with their mode; the one file left written in process is the binary blob of `an_executable_that_is_not_text_is_skipped_and_does_not_stop_the_walk`, which is not text and never run, and the held handle of `a_script_with_a_write_handle_open_on_it_is_busy_and_a_planted_one_is_not`, which is the failure that test shows; the aggregate, declared, tools, what and ask tests pass.
