# a-resumed-session-s-transcript-starts-at-its-last-compaction

A cloud session resumed in a fresh container carries its transcript over from its last compaction on: the file opens on the harness's compaction boundary, a line with no parent, and every line before it is gone. Every capture the session made before that has a `through` naming one of the lines that are gone, so `homma capture` refused the first such file it met and the session could not be captured at all, on 29 October, by the coordinator whose words most needed it.

The design now says that a transcript opening on a compaction boundary with no parent is cut, that a `through` a cut transcript lacks names a line written before the cut, and that such a capture moves no watermark: its words are not in the file, so nothing of them can be written twice. A transcript that opens anywhere else still refuses a `through` it lacks, as before.
