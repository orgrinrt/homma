# The cheap half was bundled with the hard half, and neither shipped

The previous round left `agent regen` unpatched and called it a locus question
for the record. The reasoning was that writing under `~/Dev/clause-dev` is the
gen pass's designed target and homma has no notion of whose machine it is on, so
a check there would forbid the tool its own purpose.

**That is true of one deny item and false of the other**, and the seventeenth
review took the two apart:

- `~/Dev/clause-dev` **is** the aggregation target. `~/Dev/clause-dev/homma.toml`
  ships `path = "/Users/orgrinrt/Dev/clause-dev"`. Refusing that forbids the tool
  its purpose and the question genuinely belongs to the record.
- `~/.claude` is **user scope**, is never an aggregation target, and refusing it
  costs nothing at all.

The review measured the second: ten lines checking the workspace's `.claude`
against the deny list leaves all 378 tests passing, refuses the reproduction, and
leaves an ordinary workspace writing its hooks normally.

So the shape of the mistake is not that the hard question was deferred. It is
that **an easy fix was bundled with a hard question and inherited its verdict**,
and the disclosure then justified both with an argument that only covers one.

What was reproduced with `workspace.path = $HOME`, at exit 0: two mode-`0755`
scripts written into `$HOME/.claude/hooks/`, and `$HOME/.claude/settings.json`
rewritten to register both as `PreToolUse` hooks. Deny item three verbatim, and
it installs code the harness then executes, beside the credentials the record
names.

The item-one question stays open and stays with the record.

## A guard added last round, unpinned, in the round about that guard

`under_by_components` compares components with
`eq_ignore_ascii_case` **or** a lowercase comparison. Deleting the second
disjunct leaves the whole suite green, and it is live: on this filesystem the
Kelvin sign and `kelvin`, and the Angstrom sign and `ångstrom`, are one inode,
and only that disjunct catches them.

**Sixth consecutive round shipping a guard nothing pins**, and the PR body says
every guard in the touched surface was checked by deleting it. It was not. The
sweep mutated the whole `Normal` arm at once, which caught the arm and said
nothing about either half, and the claim was written from the arm's result.

A mutation that replaces a compound condition wholesale is one mutation, not two.

## The residual was understated, and the fix is free

Both the code and the design document name Unicode normalisation as the only
thing the comparison misses. Two further classes are **case** rather than
normalisation, and `to_lowercase` misses both: `straße` against `STRASSE`, and
`Σigma` against `ςigma`. Each is one inode on this filesystem and each is
answered "not under" today.

`to_uppercase().to_lowercase()` closes both, needs no dependency, and the review
verified it. NFC against NFD stays open under either, so the `FIXME` remains, and
it is true once these two are gone rather than approximately true.

## Two tests that assert nothing, one of which hangs

`contained.rs`'s symlink-cycle test is `let _ = root.contain(...)`. The name says
"reported"; the body passes whether it reports or returns `Ok`. `contain` does
return an error today, so the assertion was available and simply was not written.

`path.rs`'s `MAX_HOPS` is unpinned in a worse way: raising it to 100000 keeps the
suite green, and **removing it hangs the suite** rather than failing it. A guard
whose absence hangs is undetectable on an unattended run, which is where this
work happens.
