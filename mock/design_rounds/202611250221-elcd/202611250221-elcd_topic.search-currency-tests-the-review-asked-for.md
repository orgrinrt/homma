The design says a refresh refused or failed gives its words, and the index is measured where it can
be. Every `Behind` the tests reach comes from an index of another model, and the one failed refresh
they reach fails its measure too. Decision: a test where the refresh is refused for a reason other
than the model, a header the writer will not take, while the measure still reads the file, and its
line says how far behind and the refresh's words. Another writer holding the file is the same arm of
the source; it waits the crate's full wait, which is ticketed apart, so the test takes the refusal
that comes at once.

test asking three places of three outcomes, each line carrying its own.
