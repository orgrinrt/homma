## CHANGE: `homma what` FROM words only TO words or `--brief <file>`

- `crates/homma-engine/src/cli.rs`: `What` takes `--brief <FILE>`, conflicting with the words;
  `--limit` is optional, five for words and `brief::LIMIT` for a brief.
- `crates/homma-engine/src/cmd/mod.rs`: a brief goes to `what::brief::run`.
- `crates/homma-engine/src/cmd/what/brief.rs` (new): `candidates` over `rank::rank_with` with a
  floor of two words; `text_of`; the content with a `=== <kind> <name>: <command> ===` heading per
  candidate; `BRIEF`; the work cut to the pass's budget; the answer `checked` by place and quoted
  span and given back by thing; `listed` where there is no extract; the key and the cache under
  `homma/brief/`; `Brief::render`; `of_workspace`, the log line, and `run`.
- `crates/homma-engine/src/cmd/what/rank.rs`: `rank_with` takes the floor; `rank` is it with
  `required`.
- `crates/homma-engine/src/cmd/what/doc.rs`: `Doc::text`, the full text a brief quotes.
- `crates/homma-engine/src/cmd/what/sources/corpora.rs`: a rule's text is its card and
  elaboration, a skill's its `SKILL.md.tmpl`.
- `crates/homma-engine/src/cmd/what/brief_tests.rs` (new): the content, the span check, the
  places, the pass off and failed, the cache, the floor, the cut, the print.

## CHANGE: a nudge row FROM a fixed text TO a fixed text or a brief

- `crates/homma-api/src/agent_nudges.rs`: `NudgeRow::brief`, refused on any strength but
  `inform`; `Nudge::brief`.
- `crates/homma-api/src/agent_nudges_tests.rs`: `a_brief_is_read_on_a_reminder_only`.
- `crates/homma-engine/src/cmd/nudge/mod.rs`: `Ctx::brief`; the call's text is the payload's
  `prompt` where there is no tool input; an `inform` row with `brief` says its brief once per text
  and again after `ttl`, and nothing for an empty text or brief; `run` briefs through
  `what::brief::of_workspace`.
- `crates/homma-engine/src/cmd/nudge/record.rs`: `RowState::briefed`.
- `crates/homma-engine/src/cmd/nudge/tests.rs`, `tests/brief.rs` (new): a stand-in brief and the
  row's tests.
